Who We Are

Two Girls, One Vision

Daring Drops started as an idea between two best friends who loved jewellery but kept finding the same problem — the most beautiful pieces would tarnish within weeks. We wanted jewellery that was actually wearable every single day.

So we started making our own. We sourced anti-tarnish materials, experimented with beads and wire-wrapping techniques, and slowly built up a collection of pieces we actually wanted to wear — bold, feminine, and built to last.

Every Daring Drops piece is handcrafted with care. We believe jewellery should be an extension of your personality — so we make pieces for bold, beautiful women who dare to stand out.
