Our Story

Made by Two Girls
Who Dare to Drop

We are Disha & Arzoo — two friends who believed jewellery should be bold, long-lasting, and truly wearable every day. Daring Drops was born from late-night craft sessions and a shared love for pieces that make a statement.

Every bracelet, bead, and earring is handpicked and crafted with care. We source anti-tarnish materials so your favourite pieces stay beautiful, not just for a season, but for years.
